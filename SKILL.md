Coarse pixel or stencil mass, maybe inside a quiet rectangle, or floating alone. Obsolete interface only as a trace, not a screen. Cold, slightly rebellious, poetic. Large empty field. Reject a specimen-label explanation, a full narrative, a smooth icon and several competing glitches.
